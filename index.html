Dictionary mark: candidates in both read-aloud modes

The reader marks the sentence being read aloud in one of two ways, and users pick which. The styling here is matched to device screenshots. Underline mode draws a 2px solid ink line from the content-box bottom (baseline + 5px) downward. Highlight mode lays a 30% yellow wash over the box, which tints the text olive, and draws a 2px pure-yellow line in the same place. User highlights use the same template in their own hue (blue here), so a saved word can also sit inside one.

Each numbered word in the read-aloud sentence is a saved dictionary word with a different candidate mark. Every mark is pure CSS on an empty 24px box whose baseline is 19px from the top, and stays in the 5px band between the baseline and the read-aloud line. Sizes are in em. The read-aloud and highlight boxes are drawn over the mark, the same way they cover the text. Literata isn’t bundled (no external fonts), so a platform serif stands in.

Underline mode

The tide had gone out in the night, and by morning the harbour at Penhallow was a field of grey mud stitched with the bright ribs of stranded boats. Mara walked the length of the sea wall twice before she found the nerve to go down the slipway, her boots slithering on the weed. Her grandfather had always said that a quiet estuary keeps its secrets badly, giving them up to anyone patient enough to wait for the lowest spring tide and stubborn enough to dig through the cold, sucking silt. She had brought his old spade, a torch she would not need until dusk, and a paper bag of sandwiches she already knew she would not eat. Behind her, somewhere up in the village, a dog barked at nothing and was told to hush.
